# DK-1063 · Device check: Compress PDF on every target ABI; seconds per page and peak memory on the 4 test devices (DK-0392)

Added by agent-0, 2026-10-08 00:36.

PdfCompress (doc_core, DK-0392) resizes and re-encodes images in pure Dart (the image package) on a pool worker. On each test device: compress a 300 dpi scan of 20+ pages at the three levels and with a size target; record seconds per page and peak memory. If a mid Android phone needs more than ~2 s per page, sample pages for the size-target search (the ponytail note in pdf_compress.dart) or move the encode to OpenCV (docs/compliance/opencv-modules.md has its config).
