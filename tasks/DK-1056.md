# DK-1056 · pdf_structure: two-column address blocks are not tables (DK-0401 finding)

Added by agent-1, 2026-10-07 23:39.

The invoice's 'Bill to: … | Invoice INV-2026-014 …' and the assessment's 'Herrn … | Datum: …' blocks come out as tables. A table needs a header row (bold or ruled) or three or more aligned columns over three or more rows. Acceptance: those two blocks are paragraphs; the invoice item table stays a table. docs/evaluations/pp-doclayout.md.
