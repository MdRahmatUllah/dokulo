# DK-1137 · S1 tablet landscape: the preview is a 3:4 portrait column with black bars; the frame fills the area

Added by agent-3, 2026-10-16 03:11.

Found in visual QA DK-0977 (24-tablet/tablet-scanner-landscape against s1_tablet_landscape.png from #1426).

Actual: s1_screen.dart sizes the preview with ScannerCamera.aspectRatio, which is 1 / controller.value.aspectRatio (always portrait, 3:4) and 3/4 by default. In landscape on a tablet (1366 x 1024) that leaves a 768 dp column with black bars left and right of it, under a top bar that only spans the column; the close button sits alone over the black.
Expected (frame): the camera fills the whole area left of the 160 dp rail (cover-crop, like the phone frame), the top bar spans that area.
Also check on a real tablet that the preview isn't rotated or stretched in landscape (the camera plugin reports the sensor ratio, not the turned one): add a Real-device checks issue if it needs one.

Acceptance: the landscape golden fills the area left of the rail; portrait unchanged; turning still keeps the scan (the DK-0652 test).
