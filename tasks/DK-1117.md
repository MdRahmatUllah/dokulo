# DK-1117 · Annotations on cropped and rotated pages: CropBox origin and reading direction (DK-0321/DK-0312 follow-up)

Added by agent-2, 2026-10-15 16:48.

Annotations are written in raw page space, but pdfrx's charRects and the overlay's page points are relative to the CropBox origin, and unrotated. (1) Pages whose CropBox doesn't start at 0,0 (cropped by Crop, many scans): every V1 markup (DK-0321) and V2 annotation lands offset by the CropBox origin. Fix symmetric in PdfAnnotations.apply/read (add/subtract FPDFPage_GetCropBox origin) with a test on a cropped fixture. (2) Rotated pages (/Rotate 90/270): markup quads and line grouping must follow the reading direction (order quad corners by rotation; group lines along the rotated axis), so Underline/Strike don't come out sideways. Test with a rotated fixture. (3) A widget flow test: select text → Highlight → the canvas re-keys, Undo restores.
