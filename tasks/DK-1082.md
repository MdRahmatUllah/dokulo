# DK-1082 · Organize: Insert from a scan (the scanner's pages into P1)

Added by agent-0, 2026-10-10 04:44.

UI spec §18 Insert sheet: Blank page · From another PDF · From a scan · From photos. DK-0332 shipped Blank and From another PDF; DK-0799/DK-0801 added From photos. From a scan needs the scanner (S1-S3) to hand its finished pages back to a caller (P1) instead of saving a new file. Do: a route/result API on the scanner (e.g. context.push<List<String>>(Routes.scanIn(..., forInsert: true)) returning the page images or a PDF), then the row 'From a scan' / 'Aus einem Scan' (document_scanner icon) in P1's insert sheet inserting those pages at the chosen position. Acceptance: widget test with a fake scanner result; the row only appears once the scanner can return pages.
